Rules (please read)

  • 1Public places only: cafe, park, library or open spaces. Never at home, in a car or in a closed room.
  • 2Talk first: all details are agreed on WhatsApp chat or call before booking.
  • 3Time limit: one session is 1 to 2 hours.
  • 4Respect and boundaries: no inappropriate talk, staring or personal questions. Respect from both sides.
  • 5If anyone is uncomfortable: the session ends right away, no arguments.
  • 6Safety: I share the place and time with a friend or family member.
  • 7Food and drinks: each pays their own, or as the client prefers.
  • 8Photo/Video: nothing is recorded or posted without your permission.
  • 9Privacy: nothing you say or share is passed on to anyone.
  • 10Cancellation: please inform at least 3 hours before.

This is not

  • Dating or matchmaking
  • Counselling or therapy
  • Personal or household work of any kind
  • Meeting at night or in isolated places
